About the studio

Maison Clay is a one-person ceramics studio in the hills of Provence. Every bowl, mug and plate is wheel-thrown, slow-fired and finished with a soft speckled glaze.

We work in small batches with regional clay and food-safe glazes. Each piece carries the small marks of the hand that made it.

Materials

Stoneware, food-safe glazes, fired at 1240°C.

Shipping

Ships from France within 2 days. Free shipping on orders over €50.
