Our story

A fairer marketplace, for the makers.

Artisan is a cooperative where independent craftspeople sell directly to a community that values handmade. No middlemen, no unfair fees.

Why we exist

Craft deserves better than a giant marketplace.

For too long, makers have had to choose between selling on platforms that take huge cuts, or building their own shop alone.

Artisan is a third way: a shared storefront owned and operated as a cooperative, where the people who make things keep most of every sale.

We're a small team based in Lyon — and 200+ makers across Europe and the Mediterranean.
